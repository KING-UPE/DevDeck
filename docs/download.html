Companion app · for your phone

DevDeck Remote

This is not DevDeck on a phone. It is a remote control for the copy running on your computer: start a dev server, watch its output and open a live preview, from anywhere.

Needs DevDeck on your computer. Install it above first and sign in with the same account — on its own this app has nothing to show.
